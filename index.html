ABOUT US

Med-Ur Supply Department (MURSD)

OUR SERVICE

Pharmaceutical Sourcing & Brokerage We connect healthcare facilities with trusted pharmaceutical suppliers, helping them source genuine medicines and essential healthcare products efficiently and reliably.

Medical Supplies Procurement We assist hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, and other healthcare facilities in sourcing the medical supplies and equipment they need from suitable suppliers.

Supplier Matching & Verification We help identify suitable suppliers based on product availability, requirements, and reliability, creating a safer and more efficient connection between buyers and suppliers.

Order Coordination From identifying the required products to coordinating supplier communication and order fulfillment, MURSD simplifies the procurement process through one trusted connection.

Supply Chain & Delivery Coordination We coordinate the movement of ordered products from suppliers to healthcare facilities, helping simplify transportation and improve visibility throughout the delivery process.

Bulk & Institutional Procuremen We support healthcare facilities with larger procurement requirements by helping them identify appropriate suppliers and coordinate their orders efficiently.

Healthcare Supply Network Through our developing Med-Network, MURSD aims to digitally connect healthcare facilities with a wider network of verified suppliers and simplify access to healthcare products across Tanzania and beyond.

🎯

Mission

MURSD connects you with trusted, compliant medicine suppliers across Tanzania and beyond, so you can access the right products at the right time without worrying about quality or delivery.

In Hospitals: We help hospitals and clinics secure reliable, on-time deliveries of genuine medicines and medical supplies by simplifying ordering and delivery.

In Community Pharmacies: We support pharmacies with consistent stock of genuine products, transparent pricing, and a safer way to work with multiple suppliers through one trusted platform.

👁

Vision

MURSD looks forward to creating a digital connection between global suppliers and medical facilities within the country through Med-Network, enabling facilities to get what they need from verified vendors while simplifying ordering and transportation from the market to their facilities.

We aim to act as a bridge across the supply chain, connecting manufacturers, wholesalers and smaller drug outlets through a trusted and efficient network.
